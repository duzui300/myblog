# 🇪🇸 Spanish Learning Notes

## 1. Episode 1.01-1.17

- **Fatal** – very bad / awful  
- **Regular** – so-so / average  

## Family & People

- **marido / esposo** – husband  
- **mujer / esposa** – wife  

## Demonstratives

- **este** – this (masculine noun)  
- **esta** – this (feminine noun)  


## Jobs & Occupations

- **ingeniero** – engineer  
- **ama de casa** – housewife  
- **fontanero** – plumber  


## Work & Daily Life

- **¿En qué trabajas?** – What do you do? (informal)  
- **¿En qué trabaja usted?** – What do you do? (formal)  
- **la fábrica** – factory  
- **Estoy jubilado / jubilada** – I am retired  
- **¿Te gusta tu trabajo?** – Do you like your job? (informal)  
- **¿Le gusta su trabajo?** – Do you like your job? (formal)  


## Hobbies & Interests

- **el deporte** – sports  
- **Me encanta ___** – I really love ___  
- **Me gusta cantar** – I like to sing  
- **salir** – to go out  
- **charlar** – to chat / talk  


## Communication in Conversation

- **¿Puedes hablar más despacio?** – Can you speak more slowly? (informal)  
- **¿Puede usted hablar más despacio?** – Can you speak more slowly? (formal)  
- **¿Cómo se escribe?** – How is it spelled?  
- **¿Cómo se dice en español?** – How do you say it in Spanish?  
- **Lo entiendo** – I understand it  
- **lo** – object pronoun (“it”)  


## Numbers & Dates

- **quinientos** – 500  
- **setecientos** – 700  
- **novecientos** – 900  
- **el siete de enero** – January 7th  


## Places

- **el mercado** – market  
- **la piscina** – swimming pool  
- **la catedral** – cathedral  
- **la fábrica** – factory  

## Asking & Giving Directions

- **Mire, señor, el museo está aquí.**  
  Look, sir, the museum is here.  

- **Siga todo recto** – Go straight ahead  
- **Doble a la derecha / izquierda** – Turn right / left  
- **lejos** – far  
- **cerca** – near  
- **El museo está lejos de aquí.** – The museum is far from here.  
- **¿Está lejos el museo?** – Is the museum far away?  
- **al lado de** – next to / beside  
- **enfrente de** – opposite  


## Food & Drinks

- **un zumo / jugo de piña** – pineapple juice  
- **una limonada** – lemonade  
- **una caña** – small beer (Spain)  
- **un vino tinto** – red wine  


## Ordering & Paying

- **para mí / para ti / para usted** – for me / for you (informal / formal)  
- **para mi amigo** – for my friend  
- **enseguida** – right away / soon  
- **¡Salud!** – cheers!  
- **¿Nos trae la cuenta?** – Can you bring us the bill?  
- **¿Nos trae ___?** – Can you bring us ___?  


## Social Interaction

- **¿Estás acompañado?** – Are you with someone?  (singular or have a partner)
- **¿Nos conocemos?** – Do we know each other?
