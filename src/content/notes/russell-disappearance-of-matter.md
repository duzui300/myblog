The main point for the philosopher in the modern theory is the disappearance of matter as a “thing”. It has been replaced by emanations from a locality — the sort of influences that characterise haunted rooms in ghost stories. All sorts of events happen in the physical world, but tables and chairs, the sun and moon, and even our daily bread, have become pale abstractions, mere laws exhibited in the successions of events which radiate from certain regions. ——Russel, 𝐴𝑛 𝑜𝑢𝑡𝑙𝑖𝑛𝑒 𝑜𝑓 𝑝ℎ𝑖𝑙𝑜𝑠𝑜𝑝ℎ𝑦,1927
